01 · Announce wings

A Focus change opens the same wings as the volume HUD: the Focus icon pops in its color left of the camera, and its name with On or Off sits right of it, then the notch returns to the music row. Tradeoff: once announced, nothing shows that a Focus is still on.

  • Pick a Focus to turn it on; pick it again to turn it off. Keys 1–5 do the same, 0 turns Focus off.
  • Turning on: the icon pops in with one ring pulse in the Focus color. Turning off: gray icon, no pulse.
  • Hides 1.6 s after the change, like the volume HUD. The camera area stays empty.
