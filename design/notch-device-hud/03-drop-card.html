03 · Drop card

Simple devices (one battery or none) use the wings from 01. Devices that report two or more batteries, like AirPods, drop a card below the notch: the full name and one ring per part, Left, Right and Case, each with its own number and label. Tradeoff: the clearest read of multi-battery devices and their low or charging part, but it covers the top 100 pt of the window for 3 s and introduces a second HUD shape to learn.

  • Card when the device reports two or more batteries (AirPods, Beats, Galaxy Buds, any other); wings otherwise. The rule is about data, not brand.
  • Card stays 3 s, wings 2 s. Both ignore the mouse: clicks reach the window under the card.
  • Wings over the card say what happened (icon left, Connected or Low right); the card says how much.
  • A part that reports nothing gets a dashed ring and “Not reported”, never 0 %.
  • Disconnects never drop a card: there is no battery to show.
