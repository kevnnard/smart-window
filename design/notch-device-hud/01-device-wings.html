01 · Device wings

A device event opens the same wings as volume and Focus: the device icon and name left of the camera, its battery right of it, then the notch returns to the music row after 2 s. AirPods fit Left, Right and Case as three short numbers. Tradeoff: consistent and never covers the window, but long names wrap to two small lines and AirPods numbers have no per-bud battery bar.

  • Shows for 2 s, then shrinks back to the music row. The window ignores the mouse, so clicks pass through.
  • Wake from sleep queues three connects; each gets its own 2 s turn, in the order macOS reports them.
  • Battery color: white above 20 %, amber at 20 % or less, red at 10 % or less, green with a bolt while charging. Low always adds the word “Low”, never color alone.
  • No battery reported: a green dot and “Connected”. Disconnect: gray icon, hollow dot, “Disconnected”.
  • Any device works: unknown types fall back to the Bluetooth icon; the battery side only shows what the device reports.
