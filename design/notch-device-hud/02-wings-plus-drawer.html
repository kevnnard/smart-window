02 · Wings plus Devices drawer

Events use the same wings as 01. The hover drawer gains a Devices tab that lists every connected device with its battery, so you can check levels at any time, not only when something connects. Hovering within 5 s of a device event opens straight on Devices. Tradeoff: the most complete answer, but it is one more tab in a 180 pt drawer, levels are only as fresh as macOS reports them, and the app has to keep tracking devices in the background.

  • Hover the notch, or use the button, to open the drawer. Esc or the chevron closes it and returns focus.
  • Event wings: 2 s, clicks pass through. Within 5 s after an event, hovering opens the Devices tab.
  • The list sorts low batteries first (red, then amber), then by when the device connected. A red dot on the tab flags a low device while you are on Music.
  • Rows never truncate the battery: long names get the ellipsis. More than five devices scroll inside the list.
  • The footer says how fresh the levels are; macOS refreshes Bluetooth battery every few minutes.
