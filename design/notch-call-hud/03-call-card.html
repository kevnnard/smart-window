  • Start: the card drops for 3 s with the app name, a big waveform and the timer, then folds into the wings of 01.
  • During: compact wings, like 01. Hovering the notch brings the card back instead of the media drawer.
  • Icons: each app shows its own icon, taken from the installed app, so it is always the official one.
  • Browser calls: when a browser holds the mic, the browser window title names the service (Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex, WhatsApp Web) with a small browser mark; an unknown site keeps the browser icon. Uses the Accessibility permission the app already has.
  • Waveform: 24 bars in the card, 7 in the wings, from your mic level (Microphone permission).
  • Hang up: the card drops again with "Call ended" and the total, 2.5 s.
  • Cost: the most motion and the biggest shape; covers the top of windows for 3 s at start and end.
